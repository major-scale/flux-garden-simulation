# Dependencies and attribution

Core dependencies: Three.js (MIT), Rapier (Apache-2.0), elkjs (EPL-2.0), and eecircuit-engine with its ngspice runtime. Consult the exact dependency distributions for their complete licenses and third-party notices; no dependency is relicensed here. TypeScript, Vite and Vitest provide build/test tooling. Lean/Mathlib support the separate formal source.

The imported ngspice RC example retains its provenance and BSD-3-Clause license in `tools/circuit-import/upstream/ngspice-rc/`.

Original project source is published for inspection without a new blanket license grant. This snapshot must not be interpreted as changing third-party terms or claiming the underlying solver implementations as original work.
